Frequently asked

Assembly Questions

What do you need to provide a quote?

Send the service location, product link or clear photos, item count, and any details about stairs, wall mounting, or limited access.

How and when do I pay?

Payment is due when the work is complete unless otherwise stated in your estimate. Pay securely through Square or with cash, and receive a receipt.

Do you provide products or replacement parts?

No. Customers provide the product, instructions, and included hardware. If something is missing or damaged, we pause and discuss the options before any added work or cost.

What happens if the product arrives damaged?

We document visible damage and pause work when it may affect safe assembly. Manufacturer defects, shipping damage, and missing parts are handled by the customer with the retailer or manufacturer.

Do you mount items to walls?

Some manufacturer-approved, non-licensed installations may be available after reviewing the item, wall type, mounting hardware, and location. We do not perform electrical, plumbing, structural, or other licensed-trade work.

Do you remove packaging?

We tidy the immediate work area and consolidate packaging. Unless specifically included in your written quote, boxes and packing material remain with the customer for recycling or disposal.

How long will my appointment take?

Timing depends on the product, item count, access, and manufacturer instructions. We provide a reasonable expectation with your quote, but complex or defective products may take longer.

What is the cancellation policy?

You may cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the appointment without a cancellation fee. See the customer policies for full details.

Is the work covered by a warranty?

Yes. Assembly or installation errors caused by our workmanship are covered for 30 days, subject to the customer policies.
